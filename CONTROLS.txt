BULLY VR for PC - controls (Touch / VR controllers, first person)
=================================================================

Left stick ............... walk (forward = where you look)
Right stick left/right ... snap turn (30 deg, change it in the VR menu > View)
Right stick up ........... jump
A ........................ sprint
B ........................ attack
X ........................ jump / back in menus
Y ........................ action
Menu button .............. pause (Start)
Left trigger ............. lock-on (L1)
Right trigger ............ R1
Stick clicks ............. L3 / R3

Right thumbrest + left stick up/down/left/right ... game Y / A / X / B
Left thumbrest + right stick ........................ d-pad
(the thumbrest is the flat touch area next to the face buttons)

Both grips + X ........... recenter the view
Both grips + Y ........... VR menu (pages: Body, Hands, View, Fight, Controls guide)

FIGHTING
- Punch with your real fists: a fast punch (about 2 m/s or more) that hits the
  opponent does real damage. Speed and strength are in VR menu > Fight.
- Fists up in front of your face = block.
- Both grips with your hands in front of you = grab (grapple).

BODY
- You see Jimmy's body; his arms follow your controllers (IK) and the body
  turns with the headset. Fine tuning (hand angle, arm reach, eye height...) is
  in VR menu > Body / Hands. Every page has "Reset this page to defaults".
- Settings are saved in BullyVR.ini in the game folder.
